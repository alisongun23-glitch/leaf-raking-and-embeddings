The game

A field holds N points. One raker must gather every point into exactly n piles, each holding at least t points.

  • Start on any point.
  • Rake (click): drag a point or a whole pile within reach k into the pile you stand on. Every loose point within l of the stroke is swept in too (a capsule 2l wide). Raking onto a loose point you stand on turns it into a pile.
  • Walk (shift-click): step onto any point or pile within k. Nothing moves.
  • Found (F): turn the point you stand on into a pile of 1. Your frontier move is walk to r, found, then rake the old pile into it.
  • Never strand yourself. Every loose point, and every pile that still needs points or a merge, must stay reachable through steps of at most k. Finished piles (at least t) may be cut off; they are frozen.

The spare points s = N − n·t are a budget: every point a pile holds beyond t spends one, and a run fails ("starvation") once the remaining points can no longer fill every pile to t.

Score = w₁·strokes/(N − n) + w₂·walk/(walk bound) + w₃·work/(work bound). Work charges every moved point its straight-line distance to its pile. Lower is better.

Toggles under Rules add push mode (push the object into any pile within k of it, ending there) and free pile placement.
